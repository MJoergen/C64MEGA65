## B. Hard-won facts about this mechanism and the write pins

### B.1 WDATA
* Idle HIGH, one active-LOW pulse per flux reversal; only the falling edge matters to the mechanism (RMC:29-41; `physical_fdd_writer.vhd:453-465`).
* Mechanism spec (TEAC FD-05HF class, cited in mega65-core `raw_bits_to_gaps.vhdl:69-72`): pulse width **0.2-1.1 us** (RMC:104-112, 262-265). mega65-core uses **988 ns** (half cell) for MFM DD, ~247 ns for raw flux, ~198 ns for DMAgic (RMC:68-76, 104-112, 113-124). AExp uses **25 cycles = 500 ns** at 50 MHz, the middle of the window, deliberately not the reference's 988 ns which grazes the top (HW:1288-1305; `physical_fdd_writer.vhd:157`).
* Launch at **cycle 50 of the 100-cycle cell** (`C_WR_LAUNCH`, `physical_fdd_writer.vhd:156`). The first version launched at cycle 10: with 140 ns precomp that left 60 ns to the cell boundary, and one larger magnitude would have computed a negative launch instant and silently stopped pulses. At the midpoint 860 ns remain either way (HW:1288-1305).
* **Registered output, no combinational path to the pin.** Paul Gardner-Stephen's glitch hunt in `sdcardio.vhdl:1727-1745` found runt lows becoming written flux; the mega65-core WDATA merge is a plain AND of two sources (RMC:256-261, 119-124). A 20 ns runt is invisible to every decode verdict; only an absolute pulse-width census catches it (HW:1674-1754).
* In mega65-core the shifter free-runs between writes, so WDATA keeps pulsing while idle: **WGATE is the only write authority; WDATA is a don't-care outside it** (RMC:97-102).

### B.2 WGATE
* Active LOW. AExp: a conjunction **evaluated every cycle** (`physical_fdd_writer.vhd:437-451`): real episode bit at the output stage AND not abort-latched AND state STREAM AND enable AND (selected OR drain-hold) AND motor AND `wr_ok`.
* Window = exactly `words x 16 x 100` cycles pin to pin, **zero lead-in, zero lead-out cells** (DEV:613-619). Measured on silicon: `0x73/0x74` = 10,376,000 cycles = 6485 x 16 x 100 (HW:1506-1530).
* mega65-core has **no WGATE timers at all**: it opens on a position event (ID-field CRC match for a sector write, index trailing edge for format) and buys erase/settle margin with written gap DATA: 23 x `$4E` (~736 us at DD) before the sync of a sector write, 6 x `$4E` flush tail, then WGATE cuts off mid-stream; deassert takes effect immediately at the drive (RMC:133-167, 160-167, 272-274). The 13 slow lead-in bytes at track start are the only "give the head time" provision (RMC:185-191).
* None of the 13 mega65-core WGATE sites is gated on /WPROT, motor, select or ready in hardware (RMC:169-176, 290-300). AExp hard-gates all of them (see C).

### B.3 Write precompensation (measured on the medium)
* AExp: **+/-7 cycles = 140 ns** (Paula `PRECOMP0`; `physical_fdd_writer.vhd:158`), 7-bit window, rule: gap-before shorter than gap-after -> early; mirror -> late; symmetric or invalid MFM -> none; window bits that reach past the episode edges get **no shift** (zero-filling would shift the first and last pulses) (DEV:621-636; `physical_fdd_writer.vhd:403-421`).
* mega65-core: magnitude 4 cycles ~99 ns, magnitude_b 8 cycles ~198 ns at 40.5 MHz, leading edge only, full case table at `mfm_bits_to_gaps.vhdl:123-189`; a write-to-read loopback exists for calibration (RMC:214-252).
* **Flux result** (precomp_step.py on Greaseweazle captures, HW:544-566): the conditioned-interval means step at the track 80/81 boundary on both heads. Core-formatted vs A500-formatted: an 8 us interval between two 4 us ones, head 1: **+220 ns vs +217 ns**; head 0: **+248 ns vs +248 ns**; a 4 us before an 8: **-82 vs -90 ns**; a 4 us after an 8: **-91 vs -79 ns**. A second step between tracks 87 and 89 appears on both disks and belongs to the drive (write-current or read-filter switch), not to the logic. The A500's absolute peak shift runs 40-60 ns above ours everywhere: the writing head, not the logic.
* The precomp counter `0x78` read 213 instead of ~20,000 on the first silicon -- because the broken encoder's class-1 gaps suppressed every shift; after the encoder fix it reads in the thousands (HW:1560-1572, 1330-1336).

### B.4 The "side flip" damage and the post-DSKBLK residue
* Paula fires DSKBLK when the HOST empties its FIFO, not when flux is on the disk (RPE:20-62). A real Paula owes one word (32 us) at that instant; ours owed 2 FIFO words + the shifter + the precomp window = **72-104 us**, three times a real Paula's (HW:1036-1046).
* X-Copy's DOS engine writes `[500 x AAAA][11 x 544 words][1 x AAAA]` = 6485 words (`DSKLEN $D955`, confirmed on silicon via `0x71`), payload ending one word before the end: **32 us of margin**, sized for a real Paula. It writes head 1 FIRST and toggles /SIDE1 (`bset #2,$bfd100`) **~25-45 us after DSKBLK**; for the head-0 write the all-drive deselect comes ~70-90 us later, inside the sacrificial word, which is why head 0 was clean (HW:1023-1066).
* Our writer treated the side change as an abort term, WGATE shut mid-cell: **85 bytes differ, all sector 10, all head 1, all offsets 510/511**, 8-16 channel cells lost, bimodal, ~65% of head-1 tracks, the damaged set moving between runs (a free-running host beat, not a fingerprint) (HW:976-1009, 720-726).
* Flux after the cut is clean but out of phase because the 207.5 ms write laps itself by ~245 words (1.04 revolutions): the head meets this write's own lead-in laid down a revolution earlier at an unrelated bit phase (HW:1068-1079). The end of a write is therefore an **off-grid interval**, not an illegal one.
* The fix is a PAIR that must never ship apart (HW:727-792; DEV:781-866): (1) `mega65.vhd:1592-1608` holds `f_selecta_o` and `f_side1_o` at their episode values while `wr_busy = '1' and wr_session = '0'` and the held select is the asserted one; STEP/DIR are deliberately NOT held (a pulse inside a hold would be destroyed, the host's cylinder counter would advance while the head stayed); (2) `physical_fdd_writer.vhd:328-331, 336, 346` (`v_hold`) stops treating select/side as abort terms once the episode level has fallen; motor, enable, tab, change, STEP and the engine abort stay live. CDC ordering is the safety property: the episode level is native to the core domain and falls FIRST, busy returns through `cdc_stable` and falls LAST, so the pin hold is a strict superset of the abort suppression (DEV:853-862).
* After the fix: the write ends **15.1 cells after sector 10's last data bit** (median; range 14.9-17.4) on both heads, 57-62 visible junctions per disk, none inside the pad word or the data (HW:568-583).

### B.5 SELECT, SIDE, STEP as the mechanism sees them
* A PC mechanism **gates its write circuitry and all its outputs on /SELn**: a deselected drive ignores WGATE silently (no abort, no tail-cut tick) and does not drive /WPROT, /CHNG or INDEX (DEV:748-752, 844-848; R2:1545-1552, the /RDY flicker). It also qualifies STEP by select (DEV:814-818).
* A mechanism switches heads the moment /SIDE1 moves (DEV:844-846). Select changes are made by E-clock-paced CIA read-modify-writes at ~1.4 us granularity, two orders above the ~161 ns CDC window (HW:793-839).
* mega65-core polarity facts: select/motor/step active low; step pulse 500 cycles ~12.35 us; head settle 8 ms busy timer; motor spin-up 1 s busy timer; a sector write cannot open WGATE until the decoder has READ the target track's header, an implicit settle guarantee (RMC:195-212).

### B.6 Density, HD media, spin-up
* `f_density` is driven at the DD level always (`mega65.vhd:1620`). mega65-core: "DD rate is too slow for the drive electronics when in HD mode", so the density line must be DD before opening WGATE for 2 us cells (RMC:266-269, 302-313). HD media was never tested; the user page says DD only (DEV:1161-1165).
* AExp ready gate: 505 ms + 2 qualified index edges (DEV:1409); mega65-core: 1 s busy timer (RMC:201-202).

### B.7 Spindle speed, cell, the drive's own controller
* MEGA65 mechanism measured from written cells: **300.2-300.5 RPM** (99,900-99,930 cells/rev); the tester's A500 296.5 RPM (101,200 cells) -- so cells-per-revolution identify WHICH drive wrote a track (writer_fingerprint.py found the A500 had rewritten track 0 of two referee disks, probably a boot-block virus) (HW:587-621).
* Cell 2.000 us DD, measured pin to pin and confirmed by originals reading back with the estimate parked at 100.00 cycles (HW:1394-1408).
* The emulated ADF spindle is 296.02 RPM (6334-word period); benign for three proven reasons; making it exactly 6250 words would remove all margin for any host that raw-copies -- **withdrawn, do not apply** (HW:941-975).
* **The mechanism's internal controller occasionally powers up wedged**: isolated knocks at ~6.3/s, nothing reads, survives Amiga reset and eject/reinsert, only a power cycle clears it; neither ROM nor HDL can generate that pattern (R2:243-279, 1096-1115). Tester note: odd knocking right after power-on -> power-cycle once; not a core bug.

### B.8 What Greaseweazle flux showed about failures
* A healthy disk, core- or Amiga-written, scans to **zero** analog patches (patch_scan.py). Media-defect patches are compact (1-2 ms), stable in angle across revolutions, and **line up in index-relative angle across tracks** (13 of 16 between 99 and 121 ms after index); X-Copy starts every track at a random angle, so write logic cannot produce that (HW:457-504).
* A never-written disk is broadband noise: tracks 0-159 statistically identical to the virgin tracks 160-163 that nothing writes (31,313 vs 31,910 transitions/rev, histogram bins within 0.7 points); the longest legal-MFM run is 10 (chance). Even an ABORTED episode leaves ~4000 legal intervals of lead-in (HW:437-460).
* Flux-tool traps: a fixed 2.000 us grid drifts off within a millisecond (cell varies up to 1.5% sector to sector) -- align in lockstep from the sync marks; one data byte is 8 raw bits per lane, odd lane at `8*i`, even lane 4096 later; virgin and DC-erased media are indistinguishable by histogram alone (HW:623-642).

### B.9 The encoder fact that nearly shipped
Minimig's `SendSector()` skips MFM clock cells ("stripped by the Amiga software anyway"): info longs had all-0 clocks (15-cell silences; limit 3), everything else `| 0xAA` all-1 clocks (2 us transitions; DD needs 4). A real clock cell is 1 iff the data cells on both sides are 0, carried across word AND sector boundaries (KS1.3 at `$FEA9E2` reads `-1(a0)`); the two sync words go out verbatim because a sync carries a MISSING clock (HW:1268-1298). Do not "fix" legality in the writer: it is a format-agnostic bit pipe that must pass protection tracks (HW:1358-1363).

## C. Design decisions that proved right, and what each prevents

| Decision | Where | Failure it prevents |
|---|---|---|
| **Shallow CDC pipe** (4-word Gray FIFO, occupancy 1-2, pop only when nearly dry, no holding register: the pop IS the reload) | DEV:555-595; `physical_fdd_wfifo.vhd:11-15`; `adf_track_engine.vhd:1258` | Bounds flux owed at DSKBLK to 3 words (~104 us); a deeper pipe would have made every post-DSKBLK host action destructive. Necessary, not sufficient (B.4). |
| **Abort latch, level for the episode** (`abort_lat`, `epi_abort` 2-FF safe) | `physical_fdd_writer.vhd:526-540`; DEV:758-770 | A returning term, re-select or re-opened drain cannot re-open WGATE mid-track; Paula's DMA survives engine aborts, so a drain-scoped session would write the remainder as a flux splat at a random position (DEV:638-650). |
| **`wr_ok` tab qualifier: 10 ms CUMULATIVE selected time**, 50 us select-settle blind window, revoke on 4-sample (80 ns) protected level or /CHNG assert EDGE | `physical_fdd_writer.vhd:161-163, 263-311` | /WPROT is meaningful only while selected; a per-deselect reset would block trackdisk (arms 2 ms after select); a 20 ns glitch must not kill a 218 ms unverified write; a /CHNG LEVEL gate would block X-Copy single-drive mode (mechanism holds /CHNG until the next step). |
| **ST_ARM re-checks live terms** (settle complete, `wp_lo = 0`), not `wr_ok` alone | `physical_fdd_writer.vhd:507-521` | `wr_ok` survives a deselect; a just-swapped protected disk would otherwise be written. |
| **Underrun detected at the cell boundary** where it happens | `physical_fdd_writer.vhd:384-391` | Waiting for the 7-cell window to empty let a 1-6 cell dry spell close and re-open WGATE: an erased hole, no abort, no reason, no count (HW:1674-1754). |
| **Read chain held in reset for the episode AND the writer's tail** (`chain_rst` includes `wr_sess_s or wr_busy_s`) | `physical_fdd_top.vhd:427-434` | A tab-blocked or aborted episode keeps WGATE shut while the disk spins; a live decoder would refill the read FIFO behind the write, pollute instruments and feed the Copylock surface mid-DMA (HW:1617-1673). |
| **Zero lead-in / lead-out** (WGATE defined at the output stage) | DEV:613-619 | Trailing erased cells would put a flux drought at the end splice; Paula starts and ends mid-stream. (Amiga-specific; see F.) |
| **Registered outputs only**, WGATE/WDATA driven by one entity | B.1 | Runt lows become written flux. |
| **No OSM write switch; the tab is the only guard** | HW:2008-2021; DEV:1167-1175 | Nothing a user can set wrong; a software switch needs a second switch for when it is in the way. The HDL still hard-gates WGATE on the conditioned /WPROT. |
| **Diag counters, per-episode + cumulative, with uptime and nonce** | DEV:1360-1397 (`0x70`-`0x7D`, `0x30`-`0x32`) | Brought up without a scope; a field session once delivered seven pasted copies of one dump (R2:716-727). |
| **Drain-scoped pin hold, only an asserted select** | DEV:800-823 | Keyed on busy alone it would freeze pins for a 207 ms track and across a held reset where trackdisk recalibrates; a deselected hold swallows steps. |
| **Episode bound on the REAL select line**, not Paula's `sel` field | DEV:654-670 | `sel = 2'd0` means both "nothing" and "df0"; an ordinary deselect gap during an ADF write bound the episode physical and committed nothing (HW:793-839). |
| **Precomp decided engine-side at the bind, crosses as one level** | DEV:626-633 | No multi-bit track value crosses clocks. |
| **Engine never parks in ST_IDLE during an episode** | DEV:684-690 | A one-poll sample of a non-existent unit would starve the pipe into an underrun. |
| **Supply > demand as the pacing argument** (Agnus 21.3 us/word vs 32 us/word) | RPE:158-190 | No track-sized buffer; a 2-3 word start threshold rides out the 3-words-then-62-us line burst. |
| **50 MHz front end, 100-cycle cells, integer 7-cycle precomp** | DEV:1121-1127 | Magnetic constants and testbench vectors transfer verbatim from the C64 precedent. |

## D. Mistakes and traps

1. **The instrument trap.** `0x71/0x73/0x74/0x77/0x79/0x7B` are per-episode and describe only the LAST track. X-Copy ends every cylinder with head 0, the clean class, so the registers could never sample a damaged episode; the first dump's "completed, 0 tail cuts, full window" was a measurement of the healthy side quoted as evidence against a defect. `0x77`'s in-flight value is sampled at session fall, after X-Copy clears DSKLEN 15-25 us later: a floor, not a ceiling (HW:1076-1093). Remedy proposed: OR-accumulating reason, cumulative tail-cut (HW:1162-1169). Also: the write bank counts since the QNICE reset, not since the `0x35` clear (HW:245-255).
2. **The sim gate graded the defect PASS.** S6 swept a deselect 25-128 us after DSKBLK and asserted that the tail-cut counter TICKS, never checking the content of the truncated region; the harness modelled the write gap-first so the last DMA word was gap by construction (HW:1094-1109). S2/S6 encoded the falsified contract "a post-DSKBLK deselect MUST cut the tail" (HW:868-880). `q_sec_ok` counted sector INSTANCES over more than one revolution: 18 of 11 with a destroyed sector 10; only `q_sec_bad = 0` caught it (HW:854-862).
3. **Spec claims falsified by bench/ROM.** Spec 6.1 described the index-synced NIBBLE engine (`$DA00/$DC00`); the DOS modes write a hard 6485 with no index wait; self-overlap 406/918 words wrong (real ~245); "residue is real-Amiga scale" false by 2-3x (HW:1110-1130). The draft's "precomp 0, byte-verified" was wrong -- KS1.3 programs 140 ns for tracks > 80 (HW:1770-1939). The first fix version froze STEP/DIR and keyed the hold on busy with comments claiming "<= ~104 us" -- false by three orders of magnitude; the second could freeze SELECT deasserted (HW:748-756, 793-812).
4. **The `sel = 00` priority-encoder ambiguity** (C, row 12): binding physical is irreversible, so one transient sample sent a whole `.adf` track into the writer while trackdisk believed it written (DEV:654-670).
5. **Asserts that watch what cannot move**: mutant (xi) unkillable in a bench with no ADF drive; mutant (viii) not behaviour-changing because two mechanisms are redundant; the in-flight assert written as "<= 4 to allow for the pipeline" where the spec says 3 let mutant (vi) survive -- the comfortable margin was the bug (HW:1674-1754). A mutant anchor that no longer matches after a fix reports HARNESS-ERROR while silently testing nothing (HW:876-880).
6. **Paula's `trackwr` is `dsklen[14]` ungated by dmaon**; modelling it as `dir_wr and dmaon` ends the DMA at the last fetch and silently stops exercising the episode model (HW:1674-1754).
7. **Read-back comparisons outside the written cylinder range are stale file content**: 77 differing bytes in 394,240 looked like a beautiful same-disk control; a genuine unwritten region differs in thousands per track (HW:233-244). `adf_compare.py` prints the distinction.
8. **X-Copy "checkdisk" is not a source comparison** (a different disk also passes); X-Copy VERIFY against a simulated ADF fails BY DESIGN (structure is re-encoded; data survives) -- do not fix (HW:1574-1583, 1219-1253). Grid digits are error codes, not counts (HW:1364-1370).
9. **Tester-side traps**: X-Copy RAM mode holds 73 cylinders in 1 MB and needs a second pass nobody ran (round 1 void); verify OFF hides bad blanks; the yellow LED protects the SD FILE, not the image (HW:393-402, 506-521).
10. **Harness hygiene**: concurrent cells sharing a workdir (`nvc -e` library collisions, `nvc -r` dumps into cwd); editing a runner script while bash executes it by byte offset; a cell run with non-matrix generics is not the matrix's cell (HW:909-940, `run_write_matrix.sh:22-31`).
11. **The read-side project was wrong twice** by promoting a mechanism before its discriminating observable was measured ("old media dies", settle theory, select-cycled re-lock); the cure was defining the observables with pre-registered predictions per hypothesis first (AUD, last paragraph; R2:302-347).

## E. The verification method that worked

**Closed-loop testbench** `CORE/sim/floppy/tb_fdd_write.vhd` (2691 lines): a line-by-line Paula-write model -> the REAL engine -> the REAL CDC FIFO -> the REAL writer -> a **live rotating flux model in 50 MHz cycle timestamps** -> `f_rdata` -> the real read chain -> real engine read service -> three verdicts: (a) a ROM-exact KS1.3 trackdisk decoder (hunt tables, SG=11 escape, literal slot walk), (b) a constant-framing real-Paula referee, (c) per-sector byte compare after an independent bit-level re-sync (`tb_fdd_write.vhd:13-16, 747-753, 1723-1762`). The model is **pre-seeded with a DIFFERENT track** and every writing scenario asserts positively that the gate opened and the old flux is gone, so a writer that never opens WGATE cannot pass by re-reading the seed (HW:1617-1673). Twelve scenarios S1-S12 plus S2x (`tb_fdd_write.vhd:48-109`): trackdisk cadence over 295.5/300/300.5/304.5 RPM x separator x framing, precomp policy at tracks 80/81/90, X-Copy cadence, the tab in three episodes plus bounce, engine aborts, every gate term, the tail sweep 25-2100 us, co-selection clicks, the df0 ambiguity, residue under all reset classes, the busy interlock, and the real X-Copy buffer with the side toggle 30 us after DSKBLK. S1 asserts the window equals `words x 16 x 100` exactly, in-flight <= 3, pulse width inside 0.2-1.1 us (`tb_fdd_write.vhd:1809-1847`).

**Independent Python twin** `CORE/sim/floppy/models/td_write_check.py` (534 lines): reconstructs every expected edge time from the serialization rules, precomp direction pinned to mega65-core's wall-clock table, and must agree with the VHDL flux model edge for edge within +/-1 cycle (38,536 = 38,536); checks V1-V5 (window length, edge set equality, no surviving pre-seed edge, nothing outside the span touched, interval histogram), `--predict` replays flux through the quantiser, `--selftest` runs its own red controls (`td_write_check.py:1-60, 134-170, 435`).

**Mutant matrix** `run_write_mutants.sh` (15 single-site edits of HDL copies: LSB-first, 99-cycle cell, dropped word, 20 ns runt, window one cell short, drain hold tied off / over-applied, ready threshold loosened, precomp sign inverted, abort on first foreign sample, abort level never set, both interlock gates, inheritance removed, precomp threshold off by one): each must be KILLED by its named cell, anchors matched token-by-token exactly once, a kill credited only if the cell is green unmutated (`run_write_mutants.sh:1-41, 311-475`). 15/15 killed on the shipped tree (HW:729-735). **Runner** `run_write_matrix.sh`: 77 cells full, ~74 s each, 25 min with `JOBS=8`; snapshots sources, per-cell workdirs, fails if a source changes mid-run (`run_write_matrix.sh:1-40, 125-166`).

**Pre-registered predictions**: the verdict table for each bench round was written BEFORE the build came back, keyed on observation -> verdict -> next step (HW:661-698, 1320-1336). The S2x red control went red against pre-fix HDL (WGATE deficit 36 cells, 2 sectors wrong) and green with the fix (HW:847-853).

**Bench protocol** (sy2002): read regression FIRST (ADF boot, trackloader, X-Copy ADF->ADF); NOS DD blanks only; originals' tabs OPEN; `M C 707C 0000` and `M C 7035 8000` at session start; write; X-Copy the disk back to a second `.adf` and `cmp`/`adf_compare.py` against the source (checkdisk is not a comparison); VA `ViewHexRawRead` at HexDump Pos `00434` for the nibble alphabet; Workbench `format` + copy + REBOOT + run `Say`; dump `M 7000 707D` in the same session; the airtight A/B puts both arms on ONE disk in ONE read pass (HW:76-187, 699-726).

## F. What is Amiga/Paula-specific and what transfers to a WD1772 1581 path

**Does NOT transfer (Amiga-specific):**
* The **raw bit pipe**: Paula hands over finished MFM words, so AExp has no encoder, no CRC, no address-mark logic. A 1581 Write Sector/Write Track must ENCODE bytes + clock with the missing-clock rule (F5 -> A1/clock-0A, F6 -> C2, F7 -> CRC) -- and the nearest proven reference for that on this very mechanism is mega65-core `mfm_bits_to_gaps.vhdl:257-273` (byte + clock byte, `last_bit0` across byte boundaries) with `crc1581.vhdl` (CRC-CCITT, init `FFFF`) (RMC:88-96, 17). The AExp encoder lesson still binds: clock cells carried across every boundary, syncs with the missing clock emitted verbatim (B.9).
* **DSKBLK and the drain hold.** The whole side-flip saga exists because Paula's completion is host-paced and leads the flux. In a WD1772 the CONTROLLER owns command completion: clear BUSY / raise INTRQ only after the writer's tail has left the output stage (wait for `busy_o` to fall), and no pin hold is needed because the DOS cannot touch SIDE/motor until the command completes. The transferable rule: **"done" is defined at the output stage, never at the FIFO pop.**
* **Episode ownership** (`wr_epi`, inheritance, foreign-selection persistence 100 us, the Paula `sel` encoder, ADF vs physical drains) is Minimig-engine machinery. The 1581 analogue is only "simulated vs physical 1581 at the same device number", already decided in the read path.
* **Zero lead-in/lead-out**: Paula starts and ends mid-stream with host-supplied gap data. A WD1772 Write Sector splices after the ID field with its own byte schedule (gap, 12 x `00`, 3 x `A1`, DAM, data, CRC, one trailing byte) and Write Track runs index to index -- mega65-core's F011 does exactly these two on this mechanism (RMC:133-193) and is the template; margins are written DATA, never timers.
* X-Copy/trackdisk cadences, WORDSYNC and the framing hold, Copylock/DSKBYTR, Paula's 2048-word FIFO as elastic buffer. For the 1772 the real-time contract is the data register + DRQ at 32 us/byte; an unserviced DRQ has DEFINED chip behaviour (LOST DATA, keep writing), unlike Paula where AExp aborts on underrun -- decide this against the datasheet, and remember issue #248 (first-byte timing).
* The 296 RPM emulated-spindle discussion, the `.adf` sector-level abstraction (verify fails by design).

**Transfers 1:1 (the 50 MHz writer front end and its discipline):**
* Cell engine 100 cycles = 2.000 us at 50 MHz (the C64 physical_1581 already runs on `c64_clk_sd_i` 50 MHz, so constants are verbatim), MSB-first serializer with FWFT reload (`physical_fdd_writer.vhd:356-397`).
* Pulse generator: 25-cycle active-low pulse launched at cycle 50 (+/- precomp), registered (`:453-465`); pulse-width census in the testbench.
* Precomp stage: 7-bit window, gap classes, early/late rule, unshifted boundary bits, counter `0x78` (`:403-421`); set the magnitude to the WD1772's own figure and VERIFY ON THE MEDIUM with `precomp_step.py` (adapt the track numbering).
* WGATE as a per-cycle conjunction with abort latch, `wr_ok` qualifier (10 ms cumulative selected, 50 us settle, 80 ns filters, /CHNG as an edge), ARM re-check of live terms, STEP/side/motor/enable terms, underrun at the cell boundary (`:263-351, 437-451, 507-540`).
* Chain reset of the read decoder for gate + tail (`physical_fdd_top.vhd:433-434`) -- for Write Sector the decoder must run right up to the ID match and again after the tail, which is exactly the AExp window.
* The Gray FIFO (`physical_fdd_wfifo.vhd` is the C64's `physical_1581_rdfifo.vhd` with 16-bit words and `rd_level_o`; one-sided reset corrupts silently, as seen in the 1581 bring-up, `:29-34`), the CDC pattern table (DEV:867-911) and the clock-pair `set_max_delay -datapath_only 20.000` in `CORE.xdc:63-91` (the C64 uses a blanket false path; AExp explains why a max-delay is preferable).
* Registered connector pins, `f_density` at DD, select-gated STEP (`mega65.vhd:1567-1622`), awareness that a deselected mechanism ignores WGATE and stops driving its status lines.
* The diag bank pattern `0x70`-`0x7D` (episodes bound / gate opened / words / WGATE window cycles / underruns / tab-blocked / in-flight + tail cut / precomp count / last track + flags / reason bits / control + precomp mode / CDC overflow) with uptime + nonce, registered falling-edge readout (DEV:912-1035, 1360-1397); make reason cumulative from day one (D.1).
* The flux instruments: `scp_tool.py` (SCP reader, PLL, index-relative timing), `patch_scan.py`, `patch_span.py`, `writer_fingerprint.py`, `structure.py` are format-light; `xcopy_tail.py`'s method (the splice is the first OFF-GRID interval after the last data bit) transfers to the 1581's post-CRC splice; `burst.py` and the AmigaDOS decoder need a 1581 sector twin.
* The method: spec audited before code, TB + twin + mutants before HDL, pre-registered verdict tables, NOS blanks, tabs open on originals, verify-on copies, an external referee (a real 1581 and a real C64 must read a core-written D81 disk, and a Greaseweazle capture must show the precomp step and the splice position).
